Milos is best seen from the sea

Many of the island's most famous places, like Kleftiko, can only be reached by boat. On a private sailing day you see the volcanic coast, the sea caves and the hidden coves at your own pace.

On board Kalypso there's shade, music, a SUP and snorkel gear. We cook a Greek lunch while you swim, and the open bar runs all day.
