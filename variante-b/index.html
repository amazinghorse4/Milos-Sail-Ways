    Good to know

    Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp, we're happy to help.

    Where does the cruise start?

    Most cruises leave from Adamas, the main port of Milos, at 10:00. When strong north wind makes the sea rough, we start from Agia Kyriaki on the south coast instead and let you know in advance.

    What is included in the price?

    The private boat and crew, breakfast, a full Greek-style lunch, an open bar with wine, beer, soft drinks, coffee and juices, plus snorkelling gear, a SUP board and a GoPro.

    How many people can join?

    The price covers up to 8 guests. Up to 10 guests can join, with €50 for each extra person. Only your group is on board.

    We have allergies or eat vegetarian. Is that OK?

    Yes. Tell us when you book and we'll adapt the menu.

    What happens if the weather is bad?

    Safety comes first. The captain may change the route to calmer water. If we have to cancel because of weather, you get a full refund.

    What should I bring?

    Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses. Everything else is on board.
