Cast off Skip the voyage, go straight to booking
10:00 · Adamas

Welcome aboard

We're Nikos and Minas, the people behind Milos Sail Ways. Step aboard Kalypso at the main port. Coffee and breakfast on deck while we cross the bay.

Tip: tap the sea and see who comes to say hello.
Morning · Klima

Colourful boathouses

The fishermen's houses of Klima and Arkoudes, painted doors right at the waterline. Have your camera ready.

Late morning · Sykia

The wild side

A rarely visited corner of the west coast. Deep turquoise water, steep volcanic rock, and silence.

Midday · Kleftiko

Jump in.

White rock arches and sea caves, reachable only by boat. Swim, snorkel, paddle the SUP, film it with our GoPro. Then a full Greek lunch on board, with wine and beer from the open bar.

Afternoon · Kalogries

One more swim

A last stop in clear water on the way back. Or a nap in the shade. Your call.

17:30 · Cape Vani → Adamas

Golden hour

We round Cape Vani and sail home in the warm afternoon light. Windy day? Then we start from Agia Kyriaki in the south: a swim at Gerakas, past Tsigrado, and you still get Kleftiko.
